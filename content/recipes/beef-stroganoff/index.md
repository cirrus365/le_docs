A stroganoff-style beef mince dish with mushrooms, crème fraîche and English mustard, served over rice.

**Servings:** 5

### Ingredients

#### Stroganoff

- 1 kg beef mince, 5% fat
- 1½ large white onions, diced
- 500 g chestnut mushrooms, sliced
- 4 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 20 ml olive oil
- A knob of butter
- 250 ml beef stock
- 500 g reduced-fat crème fraîche
- 1½–2 tbsp English mustard, to taste
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

#### To serve

- 400 g uncooked white rice

### Method

1. **Cook the rice.** Prepare the rice separately according to the packet instructions or using a rice cooker.

2. **Soften the onions.** Heat the olive oil in a large pan. Add the onions and cook gently for 12–15 minutes until soft and translucent.

3. **Cook the mushrooms.** Add the garlic, followed by the butter and mushrooms. Cook for about 5 minutes, then transfer the onion and mushroom mixture to a bowl.

4. **Brown the beef.** Add the beef mince to the pan. Break it apart and cook until browned and cooked through.

5. **Combine.** Return the onion and mushroom mixture to the pan and stir into the beef.

6. **Make the sauce.** Pour in the beef stock, then stir in the crème fraîche and English mustard. Heat gently until everything is combined and hot throughout.

7. **Season.** Taste and adjust the salt, pepper and mustard as needed.

8. **Serve.** Divide the cooked rice between five bowls or containers, then spoon the stroganoff over the top.

### Notes

- Start with 1½ tablespoons of English mustard and add more to taste.
- The butter quantity is approximate: use a small knob.
- Serve the rice and stroganoff in layers rather than mixing them together.
