Creamy meatballs and orzo cooked together in one pan with chicken stock, crème fraîche, cheese and peas.

**Servings:** 5

### Ingredients

#### Meatballs

- 750 g beef mince
- 1 egg
- Salt, to taste
- Black pepper, to taste
- Dried herbs, optional
- A little olive oil, for frying if needed

#### Orzo and sauce

- 375 g orzo
- 25 g Grana Padano or another Italian hard cheese, grated
- 150 ml crème fraîche
- 100 g frozen peas
- 5 g garlic purée
- Prepared chicken stock — the video does not specify the volume
- Juice of ½ lemon, optional
- Boiling water, as needed during cooking

### Method

1. **Make the meatballs.** Mix the beef mince, egg, a little salt, plenty of black pepper and optional dried herbs. Shape into evenly sized meatballs, making a number that divides equally between five portions.

2. **Brown the meatballs.** Heat a large, lidded pan over a high heat. Add a little olive oil if needed, then add the meatballs. Leave undisturbed for 3–5 minutes before checking the colour. Turn and brown the other side, then remove from the pan. They will finish cooking in the sauce.

3. **Add the garlic.** Reduce the heat and let the pan cool slightly before adding the garlic. If using freshly chopped garlic instead of purée, cook gently for about a minute without letting it burn.

4. **Build the sauce.** Add the orzo, prepared chicken stock, crème fraîche, grated cheese and optional lemon juice. Stir until evenly combined.

5. **Simmer.** Return the meatballs to the pan. Bring to a gentle simmer, cover and cook for about 15 minutes. Check occasionally and add boiling water if the mixture is becoming too dry.

6. **Add the peas.** Stir in the frozen peas, replace the lid and cook for another 5 minutes, until the orzo is tender and the meatballs are cooked through.

7. **Portion.** Divide the meatballs and orzo evenly between five containers.

### Notes

- Uses 5% fat beef mince, but you can 10% fat or higher for flavour.
- Grana Padano, Parmesan or Pecorino can be used for the cheese.
- Breadcrumbs are not used in the meatballs.
- The chicken stock and cheese contribute salt, so season the meatballs lightly.
- The stock volume is unspecified in the source; monitor the pan and add boiling water as needed.
