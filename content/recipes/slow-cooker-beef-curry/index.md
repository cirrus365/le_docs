Tender beef in a coconut curry sauce with onions, garlic, ginger and warming spices. Fry the onions and spices first for extra flavour, then let the slow cooker finish the cooking.

#### Cooking time

- **Low:** 7–8 hours.
- **Medium:** 3–4 hours, if your slow cooker has this setting.
- Cook until the beef is tender.

#### Ingredients

- 2 onions, white or red, finely chopped
- Coconut oil, for frying — quantity not specified
- 25 g garlic paste
- 25 g ginger paste
- 2 tablespoons mild curry powder
- 1 teaspoon garam masala
- 50 g tomato paste
- 1 kg lean beef, diced
- Plain flour or cornflour, for thickening — quantity not specified
- 400 ml full-fat coconut milk

**To serve:**

- Fresh coriander
- Chilli flakes, to taste
- Rice, optional

#### Method

1. **Soften the onions.** Heat a little coconut oil in a large frying pan over medium heat. Add the chopped onions and cook for a few minutes until softened.

2. **Add the aromatics and spices.** Stir in the garlic paste, ginger paste, curry powder and garam masala.

3. **Cook the tomato paste.** Add the tomato paste and cook, stirring, for a couple of minutes.

4. **Add the beef.** Stir in the diced beef and cook for a few more minutes until it begins to brown.

5. **Transfer to the slow cooker.** Add the beef and onion mixture to the slow cooker. Stir in your chosen thickener, then add the coconut milk and mix well.

6. **Slow cook.** Cover and cook on **low for 7–8 hours**, or **medium for 3–4 hours** if your appliance has that setting, until the beef is tender.

7. **Serve.** Stir the curry and serve on its own or with rice. Finish with fresh coriander and chilli flakes.

#### Notes

- **Thickener:** No quantity is specified for the flour or cornflour, so an exact measurement cannot be provided.
- **Coconut milk:** Use full-fat coconut milk; light coconut milk may split during cooking.
- **Shortcut:** You can skip the frying stage and add the ingredients directly to the slow cooker, although the curry will have less depth of flavour.
- **Garlic and ginger:** You can replace the separate pastes with 50 g combined garlic and ginger paste.
- **Slow cooker settings:** The shorter cooking time is for a medium setting, not a verified high-setting alternative. Follow your appliance’s guidance if it only has low and high settings.
