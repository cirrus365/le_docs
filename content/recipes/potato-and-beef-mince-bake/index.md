Dinner in 20 Minutes! 🔥 Old German Recipe, a layered potato bake with a beef and vegetable filling, a golden cheese topping and a fresh salad on the side.

#### Cooking time

- **Boiling potatoes:** About 20 minutes.
- **Baking:** 20 minutes at 180°C.
- Additional time is needed to prepare and cook the filling.

#### Ingredients

**For the beef filling:**

- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 30 g butter
- 500 g beef mince
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 1 red capsicum, diced
- 1 tomato, diced
- 1 carrot, finely diced
- About 30 g tomato paste
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon ground black pepper
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 1 teaspoon Italian herbs
- A small bunch of fresh parsley, finely chopped

**For the potato layers:**

- 4–5 large potatoes, peeled and cubed
- ½ tablespoon salt, for the cooking water
- About 30 g butter, melted
- 2 large eggs
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon ground black pepper
- 30 g plain flour

**For assembling:**

- A little olive oil, for greasing
- 150 g Emmental cheese, grated

**For the salad:**

- 2 handfuls mixed lettuce leaves, chopped
- 1 yellow capsicum, diced
- 1 red onion, thinly sliced
- 1 tomato, sliced
- 1 cucumber, sliced
- 1 ball mozzarella, cut into small pieces
- A small bunch of fresh parsley, finely chopped

**For the dressing:**

- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon ground black pepper
- Juice of ½ lemon
- 1 teaspoon mustard

#### Method

##### 1. Prepare the eggs

Whisk the eggs with the ½ teaspoon salt and ½ teaspoon black pepper for the potato mixture. Set aside.

##### 2. Cook the beef filling

1. Heat the vegetable oil and butter in a large frying pan.
2. Add the beef mince. Break it up with a spatula and cook until it begins to brown.
3. Add the onion and cook for 4–5 minutes, until softened.
4. Stir in the garlic and cook, stirring frequently.
5. Add the red capsicum and cook for another 4–5 minutes.
6. Stir in the diced tomato, followed by the carrot. Cook for 7–10 minutes, stirring occasionally.
7. Add the tomato paste and cook for 2–3 minutes.
8. Stir in the salt, black pepper, paprika and Italian herbs. Cook for another 4–5 minutes, until the vegetables are tender and the mince is cooked through.
9. Stir in the parsley and remove from the heat.

##### 3. Prepare the potato mixture

1. Place the potatoes in a saucepan and cover with water. Add the salt for the cooking water.
2. Bring to the boil and cook for about 20 minutes, or until fork-tender.
3. Drain thoroughly and mash until smooth.
4. Mix in the melted butter.
5. Stir in the beaten egg mixture.
6. Add the flour and mix until evenly combined.

##### 4. Assemble and bake

1. Preheat the oven to **180°C**.
2. Lightly grease a baking dish with olive oil.
3. Spread half the potato mixture evenly over the bottom.
4. Spoon the beef filling over the potato layer and spread to the edges.
5. Cover with the remaining potato mixture and smooth the surface.
6. Sprinkle the grated Emmental evenly over the top.
7. Bake for **20 minutes**, until the cheese is melted and golden and the centre is piping hot.
8. Allow to cool slightly before cutting into portions.

##### 5. Make the salad

1. Combine the lettuce, yellow capsicum, red onion, tomato, cucumber, mozzarella and parsley in a large bowl.
2. In a small bowl, whisk together the olive oil, salt, black pepper, lemon juice and mustard.
3. Pour over the salad just before serving and toss gently.

##### 6. Serve

Serve the warm potato and beef mince bake with the dressed salad alongside.

#### Notes

- **Servings and baking dish size:** Not specified.
- **Oven setting:** Fan or conventional is not specified.
- **Tomatoes:** Use two in total—one for the beef filling and one for the salad.
- **Flour:** Use the listed weight of 30 g for consistency; tablespoon measurements can vary.
