A hearty cottage pie with a beef and vegetable filling, mashed potato topping and golden cheddar crust.

**Servings:** 5

### Ingredients

#### Beef filling

- 1 kg beef mince, 5% fat
- 1½ large white onions, finely diced
- 1 carrot, finely diced
- 4 celery stalks, finely diced
- 4 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 20 ml olive oil
- 2 tbsp tomato purée
- 20 g plain flour
- 1 litre beef stock
- 150 ml red wine
- 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 2 bay leaves
- Black pepper, to taste

#### Potato topping

- 1.2 kg potatoes, peeled and cut into evenly sized pieces
- 50 g butter
- 100 ml semi-skimmed milk
- 100 g cheddar cheese, grated

### Method

1. **Soften the vegetables.** Heat the olive oil in a large pan. Add the onions, carrot and celery, then cook gently for 15–20 minutes until softened.

2. **Add the garlic and beef.** Stir in the garlic, then add the beef mince. Break it apart as it cooks and continue until browned.

3. **Make the gravy.** Stir in the tomato purée and flour. Add the red wine, beef stock, Worcestershire sauce and bay leaves. Season with black pepper.

4. **Reduce the filling.** Simmer for 35–45 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the gravy has thickened.

5. **Cook the potatoes.** Meanwhile, boil the potatoes until tender. Drain, then mash with the butter and milk until smooth.

6. **Assemble.** Heat the oven to 180°C. Remove the bay leaves from the filling and transfer it to a baking dish. Spread the mashed potato over the beef, then sprinkle the grated cheddar on top.

7. **Bake.** Bake for 35 minutes, then increase the oven temperature to 200°C for a further 5 minutes to crisp and brown the topping.

8. **Serve.** Divide into five portions.

### Notes

- The beef stock contributes salt, so taste the filling before adding any extra.
- Oven temperatures are given without a specified fan or conventional setting.
