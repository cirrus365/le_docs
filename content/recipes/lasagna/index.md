A rich meat lasagna layered with ricotta and finished with a golden mozzarella and Parmesan topping.

**Baking dish:** Approximately 23 × 33 cm (9 × 13 inches)

### Ingredients

#### Meat sauce

- ¾ lb Italian sausage, casings removed
- ¾ lb beef mince
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 7 garlic cloves, minced
- 170 g tomato paste
- 794 g crushed tomatoes
- 425 g tomato sauce
- 1 tbsp granulated sugar
- 1 tbsp Italian seasoning
- 1 tsp dried basil
- 1½ tsp salt
- ¾ tsp cracked black pepper
- ½ tsp chilli flakes, optional
- Lemon zest, to taste, optional

#### Ricotta filling

- 425 g ricotta
- 1 large egg
- 1 tbsp finely chopped fresh parsley
- ½ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp cracked black pepper
- Lemon zest, oregano or basil, optional

#### Assembly

- Approximately 12 lasagna sheets, depending on their size
- 450 g fresh mozzarella, sliced
- 26 g freshly grated Parmesan

### Method

1. **Brown the meat.** Heat a large, heavy-based pot over medium-high heat. Add the sausage and beef mince. Cook for 6–8 minutes, breaking the meat into small pieces as it browns.

2. **Add the aromatics.** Stir in the onion and garlic. Cook for about 5 minutes, stirring frequently, until softened.

3. **Make the sauce.** Stir in the tomato paste until incorporated. Add the crushed tomatoes, tomato sauce, sugar, Italian seasoning, dried basil, salt, pepper and optional chilli flakes.

4. **Simmer.** Reduce the heat to medium-low and simmer for approximately 25 minutes, until thickened. Stir in the optional lemon zest at the end.

5. **Prepare the pasta.** Bring a large pot of salted water to the boil. Cook the lasagna sheets until just al dente, leaving them slightly undercooked. Drain and rinse with cold water to stop them cooking further.

6. **Mix the filling.** Combine the ricotta, egg, parsley, salt and pepper in a bowl. Stir in any optional lemon zest or additional herbs.

7. **Prepare the oven.** Heat the oven to 190°C (375°F).

8. **Build the first layer.** Spread 2 cups of meat sauce over the bottom of the baking dish. Cover with a layer of pasta, then spread half the ricotta filling over it.

9. **Build the second layer.** Add another 2 cups of meat sauce, followed by a layer of pasta and the remaining ricotta filling.

10. **Finish the layers.** Spread half the remaining meat sauce over the ricotta. Add a final layer of pasta, then cover with the remaining sauce. Arrange the mozzarella on top and sprinkle with Parmesan.

11. **Bake covered.** Cover the dish with aluminium foil and bake for 30 minutes.

12. **Brown the topping.** Remove the foil and bake for another 15 minutes, until bubbling and golden. For extra colour, optionally finish under the grill for 2–3 minutes, watching carefully.

13. **Rest.** Leave the lasagna to stand for 15 minutes before slicing and serving.

### Notes

- The pasta quantity is approximate; use enough sheets for three layers in your dish.
- Use tomato cooking sauce, not table tomato sauce or ketchup.
- The oven temperature does not specify a fan or conventional setting.
