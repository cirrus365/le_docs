A quick one-pan breakfast hash: potatoes fried until golden, then cooked with chorizo, shallots, garlic, butter and spices, finished with ketchup, hot sauce and chives, and served with scrambled eggs.

#### Cooking time

- **Potatoes:** Fry until nicely coloured, then cook for a further 4 minutes 23 seconds after the chorizo and seasoning go in.
- **Total:** About 10 minutes.

#### Ingredients

- Cubed potatoes — quantity not specified
- Oil, for frying — quantity not specified
- Diced chorizo — quantity not specified
- Shallots (or onion), diced — quantity not specified
- Garlic, minced — quantity not specified
- Butter — quantity not specified
- Seasoning: paprika, black pepper, oregano and cumin — quantities not specified
- Spicy ketchup — quantity not specified
- Hot sauce, to taste — optional
- Fresh chives, chopped
- Scrambled eggs, to serve

#### Method

1. **Fry the potatoes.** Heat oil in a frying pan and add the cubed potatoes. Fry, tossing occasionally, until they have a nice golden colour.

2. **Add the chorizo and aromatics.** Add the diced chorizo, shallots and minced garlic to the pan and stir through.

3. **Add butter and seasoning.** Add the butter and the paprika, black pepper, oregano and cumin, and mix well.

4. **Cook through.** Cook for another 4 minutes 23 seconds, stirring so everything cooks evenly and the potatoes finish browning.

5. **Finish the hash.** Squeeze the spicy ketchup over the potatoes and stir to coat. Add hot sauce to taste, then scatter over the chopped chives.

6. **Serve.** Serve straight from the pan with creamy scrambled eggs alongside.
